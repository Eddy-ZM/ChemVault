# TestFlight Beta App Description Draft

Draft only. Needs Apple review and legal review.

ChemVault is a beta scientific workspace for testing account, file, mail, molecule, and AI extraction workflows. This TestFlight build may be unstable, incomplete, or changed during testing.

Do not use this beta for production-critical, regulated, confidential, clinical, safety-critical, or irreplaceable data unless you are authorized to do so. AI and scientific extraction results may be inaccurate and require human review before use.

Data submitted through the app may be processed by ChemVault services and third-party providers such as Cloudflare, Apple, Resend or SMTP providers, and AI providers where features are enabled. See the ChemVault Privacy Policy before testing.

Feedback and security reports should use the published ChemVault support or security reporting channel.
