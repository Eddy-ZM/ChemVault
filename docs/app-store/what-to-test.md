# TestFlight What To Test Draft

Draft only. Needs Apple review and product review.

Please test:

- sign in, sign out, and session persistence;
- account profile and account deletion request entry;
- file upload, preview, download, sharing, and permission checks;
- mail send, receive, search, delete, and app-password flows where enabled;
- molecule search, SMILES input, 3D generation, local library, and export;
- AI extraction upload, job status, review, correction, and export;
- notification opt-in, delivery, and opt-out controls;
- privacy, terms, support, and security/abuse links;
- offline states, failed requests, and retry behavior.

Do not upload sensitive personal information, confidential files, regulated data, or content you do not have permission to process.
