ChemVault Privacy Policy

Draft only. This document is not legal advice and must be reviewed before commercial launch.

This page is synchronized from docs/legal/privacy-policy.md for public review.

This draft is a transparency notice, not a certification that ChemVault satisfies every privacy, security, App Store, email, research, export-control, or sector-specific legal requirement.

User controls Request account deletion Request data export Security / abuse reporting Terms of Service

Information ChemVault may collect

ChemVault may process account and profile information, login and session data, email content and metadata, uploaded files, AI inputs and outputs, molecule data, notification data, contact or lead forms, device/browser information, IP addresses, logs, and Apple TestFlight or App Store information where applicable.

Sensitive and regulated data

ChemVault is not currently documented as approved for protected health information, clinical care, regulated submissions, GxP records, export-controlled data, payment card storage, school records, children's data, or other specially regulated information unless a separate written agreement or deployment notice expressly says so.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate accounts, permissions, mail, file storage, AI extraction, notifications, support, commercial interest forms, abuse prevention, audit logging, service reliability, and legal or security response workflows.

AI and scientific data

AI features may process submitted text, files, extracted content, prompts, molecule data, or outputs through third-party AI services. Do not submit sensitive personal information, confidential data, or content you do not have permission to process. AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for important decisions and should be reviewed before use.

Email and files

ChemVault Mail may process message bodies, headers, recipients, subjects, attachments, delivery metadata, app password activity, and audit events. File services may process uploaded files, file names, sizes, MIME types, storage keys, checksums, sharing tokens, previews, and activity logs.

Third-party services

ChemVault may use Cloudflare, Resend or SMTP providers, OpenAI or other AI providers, Apple, GitHub, Supabase, OAuth providers, and payment processors if billing is enabled. Provider configuration, contracts, retention settings, subprocessors, and international transfer terms need legal review.

Retention and user rights

Retention periods are not final. ChemVault should keep data only as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and support. Users may request deletion or export through the links on this page, but cross-service automation is still a pending request baseline and may require identity verification, administrator review, and manual processing.

Security

ChemVault uses reasonable safeguards such as authentication, role checks, hashed tokens, encrypted secrets where implemented, audit logs, and Cloudflare infrastructure. No security measure is absolute, and ChemVault cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, data loss, provider outages, vulnerabilities, or configuration mistakes will never occur.

Children and education users

ChemVault is not currently documented as directed to children. Use by minors, schools, universities, or education programs may trigger additional obligations and needs legal review.

Contact

Contact: contact@chemvault.science. Security and abuse reports can also use forms.chemvault.science/security-report.
