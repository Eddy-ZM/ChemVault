Roadmap

A staged path from public portal to research infrastructure.

The roadmap is intentionally modular so each layer can mature without blocking the existing static public site.

Foundation

Maintain the public chemistry index, compound search, research pages and static deployment pipeline.

Extraction

Develop document extraction experiments for papers, PDFs, lab reports and structured scientific records.

Knowledge layer

Connect compounds, methods, literature claims, experimental conditions and source provenance.

Collaboration

Introduce controlled file, notification and correspondence layers for research-oriented project workflows.
