Platform Capabilities

ChemVault is designed as a modular research infrastructure platform for chemistry data, scientific documents, compound information and AI-assisted knowledge workflows.

Core modules Compound Search Paper-to-Database Literature Graph Research Workspace
Capability Architecture

Independent modules with a shared knowledge layer.

Each capability can mature on its own while contributing to the same scientific data model: entities, sources, methods, claims, observations and project context.

Active

Compound Search

A fast chemical compound search interface for looking up chemical names, formulas, identifiers, molecular data and related compound information.

Use cases

Name, formula, CAS-style identifier and PubChem-oriented lookup for chemistry records.

Future direction

Clearer compound metadata, stronger identifier handling and links from molecular records into document-derived research evidence.

Platform direction

The platform is designed to connect document extraction, chemical search and research workflow intelligence without forcing the public site into server-only architecture.

The current implementation remains static and Cloudflare-friendly, while future services can attach through APIs, D1-backed records, private file infrastructure and controlled research tools.
