Scientific tooling for structured research knowledge.

Platform direction

The platform is designed to connect document extraction, chemical search and research workflow intelligence without forcing the public site into server-only architecture.

The current implementation remains static and Cloudflare-friendly, while future services can attach through APIs, D1-backed records, private file infrastructure and controlled research tools.
