FAQ

Beta access notes.

The current access model is intentionally conservative and does not represent unavailable capabilities as purchasable.

Is payment live?

No. This page does not offer checkout or collect payment. Commercial packaging will follow validated usage and lifecycle readiness.

Is AI Paper Search live?

It is an early-access preview. Availability and plan limits are shown explicitly, and the product does not fabricate paper data.

Can teams use shared workspaces?

Team pilots are scoped individually while authenticated organization data and shared-workspace controls are validated.

How are permissions enforced?

Protected actions require server-side identity and resource authorization; beta labels in the interface do not grant access by themselves.
