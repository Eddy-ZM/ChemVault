What deletion may affect

Deletion may affect ChemVault Mail, Files, Docs, Notifications, Extract, Apple apps, sessions, OAuth connections, app passwords, API keys, uploaded content, AI outputs, and logs. Cross-service automation is a baseline implementation and may require manual handling.

Submitting this form does not immediately delete data or make deletion irreversible. The request enters pending status first.
